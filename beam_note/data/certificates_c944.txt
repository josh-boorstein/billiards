Two adjacent periodic cylinders in an irrational right triangle (certificate data)
=================================================================================

Triangle: right angle at O; the smaller acute angle alpha is at B, with cos(2 alpha) = (sqrt(7) - 1)/4,
alpha = 32.8532 degrees (alpha/pi irrational). The leg OB is adjacent to alpha. Sides are named by letters:
  A = the leg OA,  B = the leg OB,  H = the hypotenuse AB.
Coordinates: O = 0, A = 1, B = i*tan(pi/2 - alpha)   (so OA = 1; heights on OB are fractions of |OB|).

Both cylinders lie in the invariant surface of the direction perpendicular to OB.

C  (non-perpendicular, period 944): a strip of its paths leaves OB between the heights
     0.1007261889 and 0.1017763495, at the angle 0.848062079 (radians) from the inward normal of OB,
     turned towards B. No path of C meets a side at a right angle.
P  (perpendicular, period 7338, i.e. a perpendicular orbit of 3669 reflections each way): a strip leaves OB
     between the heights 0.0982543307 and 0.1007261889, in the same direction. Its paths meet OB at a right angle.
The two strips are adjacent: the common boundary line passes through a vertex, so C and P share a boundary
saddle connection, with C on one side and P on the other.

Each word below is the cyclic sequence of sides hit by every path of the cylinder, starting with the first
side hit after leaving OB at the strip above and ending with that departure (B). A word is the word of a
periodic cylinder iff its unfolding g_1 ... g_n (g_i = reflection in side w_i) is a translation by some
beta != 0 and some line parallel to beta separates the endpoints of every crossed edge; both conditions were
checked in exact arithmetic in Q(2 cos alpha), and the two cylinders' boundary lines were checked to coincide
exactly. (Code: the Mirror A deposit, https://github.com/josh-boorstein/billiards, mirror_A/ and lib/mirror.py,
which certifies C; P was certified with the same routine.)
